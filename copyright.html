Copyright Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Artists upload their own music to Muse, and every upload must be theirs to publish. This page explains how to tell us that content on Muse infringes your copyright, how an uploader can respond, and what happens to users who infringe repeatedly. It expands on Section 4 of our Terms of Service, which it forms part of.

1. Before You Send a Notice

  • Only the copyright owner, or someone authorised to act for them, can send a copyright notice.
  • Check first whether the use might be licensed or otherwise allowed by law.
  • For anything other than copyright — such as offensive content, impersonation, or spam — use Report in the App instead. It's faster.

2. How to Report Infringement

Email us at kyimoemin0@gmail.com with the subject line "Copyright notice", and include:

  1. Your name and contact details (email address, and a postal address or phone number), and your signature (typed is fine);
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you believe is infringed, such as the song title and the recording or composition it belongs to;
  3. The song, album, or artist on Muse you believe infringes it, with enough detail for us to find it (for example, the artist name and song title as shown in the App);
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law; and
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act for the owner.

If your notice is missing something we need, we may ask you for it before we act.

3. What Happens Next

  • We review every complete notice. We may unpublish the content while we review it.
  • If we find the content infringes, we take it down.
  • We let the uploader know that their content was taken down and why. We may share your notice with them, including your name and contact details, so they can respond or contact you.
  • A takedown may count as a strike against the uploader (see Section 5).

4. Counter-Notices

If your content was taken down and you believe that was a mistake — for example, you own the work or have a licence to publish it — you can send a counter-notice to kyimoemin0@gmail.com with the subject line "Copyright counter-notice", including:

  1. Your name, the email address of your Muse account, and your signature (typed is fine);
  2. The song or album that was taken down;
  3. An explanation of why you believe the takedown was a mistake, such as proof of ownership or of your licence; and
  4. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you believe in good faith the content was taken down by mistake or misidentification.

We review counter-notices and may restore the content. We may share your counter-notice with the person who sent the original notice.

5. Repeat Infringers

Muse terminates the accounts of users who repeatedly infringe copyright. A takedown for copyright infringement may count as one strike against the uploader, under the same strike system as in Section 3.4 of our Terms of Service:

  • First strike: a warning.
  • Second strike: your account is suspended.
  • Third strike: your account is banned.

We may suspend or ban an account immediately, without going through strikes, for serious or repeated infringement — for example, uploading music that is plainly someone else's. A ban may also unpublish all of the user's uploads.

6. False or Abusive Notices

Sending a notice or counter-notice you know to be false may make you liable for the harm it causes. We may disregard notices or counter-notices that are incomplete, made in bad faith, or intended to harass.
