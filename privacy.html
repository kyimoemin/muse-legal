Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Muse ("Muse", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Muse mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Summary

  • You can browse and play music as a guest, without creating an account.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers, or advertising ID.
  • We collect only what's needed to run the service: account details when you sign up, content you choose to upload, listening activity to power the service, and payment details if you set up payouts.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account information

When you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Email address, username, password, and gender (provided at sign-up).
  • If you sign in with Facebook, we receive an authentication token from Facebook to create or access your account.
  • Optional profile details you add, such as an artist name and a profile photo.

2.2 Listening activity

To operate the service (for example, to power play counts and improve content), the App reports the song identifier and how long a track is played, along with a timestamp. This is collected for all users, including guests. For guests, this information is not linked to a personal identity; for signed-in users, it is associated with your account.

2.3 Content you upload

If you are an artist, the App lets you upload audio files, cover images, and related metadata (such as song titles, album names, and genre). A profile photo you choose is also uploaded. To select these, the App requests access to your device's photo library (for images) and file picker (for audio). We access only the specific files you choose.

2.4 Payment information

If you set up payouts or manage payment methods, we collect the payment details you enter, such as account name, account number, and payment method type. These are transmitted securely to our backend to process withdrawal requests. We do not store full card data on your device, and in this version in-app deposits and purchases are not enabled.

2.5 Information stored on your device

The App stores some data locally on your device to function, including:

  • Authentication tokens (stored in encrypted on-device storage).
  • App preferences (such as theme, sort order, and notification setting), recent search terms, upload drafts, and a queue of pending listening activity.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, camera, device identifiers, or advertising ID.
  • We do not sell your personal information or share it with data brokers.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your data. We may share information only:

  • With service providers that host our backend and store content on our behalf, under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons, if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the service.
  • Public content: songs, albums, artist names, and profile photos that you publish as an artist are visible to other users by design.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time (see Section 9). Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, or legitimate operational reasons.

7. Security

Data transmitted between the App and our servers is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted on-device storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children's Privacy

Muse is not directed to children under the age required by your local law to consent to data processing (for example, 13 in the United States). We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your account data by contacting us.
  • Account deletion: email us and we will delete your account and associated personal data.
  • Depending on your region (e.g., EEA/UK or California), you may have additional rights such as data portability or objection to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these.

10. International Users

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own. By using the App, you consent to such transfer, subject to appropriate safeguards.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App.
