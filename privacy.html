Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Muse ("Muse", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Muse mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. Your use of the App is also governed by our Terms of Service.

1. Summary

  • You can browse and play music as a guest, without creating an account.
  • The free version of Muse shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads (see Section 2.6). You can limit this, and Muse Plus subscribers see no ads.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, we do not sell your data, and we do not collect your precise location or contacts.
  • We collect only what's needed to run the service: account details when you sign up, content you choose to upload, identity details if you apply to become an artist, listening activity to power the service, subscription status if you buy Muse Plus, and payment details if you set up payouts.
  • You can delete your account from within the App at any time (see Section 9).

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account information

When you create an account or sign in, we collect:

  • Email address, username, password, and gender (provided at sign-up).
  • Optional profile details you add, such as an artist name, a profile photo, and a phone number. The phone number is not collected at sign-up; you may choose to add it later when editing your profile, and you can leave it blank or remove it at any time. If you apply to become an artist, we ask for one on the application (Section 2.7).

2.2 Listening activity

To operate the service (for example, to power play counts, share ad revenue with artists according to how long their music is played, and improve content), the App reports the song identifier and how long a track is played, along with a timestamp. This is collected for all users, including guests. For guests, this information is not linked to a personal identity; for signed-in users, it is associated with your account. The App also keeps a count of playing time on your device to limit how often ads are shown.

2.3 Content you upload

If you are an artist, the App lets you upload audio files, cover images, and related metadata (such as song titles, album names, and genre). A profile photo you choose is also uploaded. To select these, the App requests access to your device's photo library (for images) and file picker (for audio). We access only the specific files you choose.

2.4 Payment information

If you are an artist and set up payouts, we collect the payout details you enter, such as account name, account number, and payment method type. These are transmitted securely to our backend to process withdrawal requests.

If you subscribe to Muse Plus, payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your card or bank details. The store sends us a purchase record (such as a transaction identifier, the product you bought, and its renewal date), which we use to verify the purchase and turn Muse Plus on for your account. Apple's and Google's privacy policies govern the payment itself.

2.5 Information stored on your device

The App stores some data locally on your device to function, including:

  • Authentication tokens (stored in encrypted on-device storage).
  • App preferences (such as theme, sort order, and notification setting), recent search terms, upload drafts, a queue of pending listening activity, and your ad consent choices.

2.6 Advertising

If you use Muse without a Muse Plus subscription (including as a guest), the App shows occasional full-screen ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, limit, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK in the App collects and sends to Google:

  • Your device's advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, if you allow tracking; Android Advertising ID on Android) and other device identifiers.
  • IP address, from which Google may estimate your approximate (not precise) location.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, and language.
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped.

Your choices:

  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, the App shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) where you can accept or decline personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time from the App's privacy settings.
  • iOS: the App asks for your permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, your IDFA is not shared.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • If you decline consent or tracking, you still see ads, but they are non-personalised (based on the context rather than your profile).
  • Muse Plus subscribers see no ads; the App does not load the ad SDK or make ad requests for them.

Google's use of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

2.7 Artist applications

To upload music you apply to become an artist in the App. Besides the artist name and optional description you enter, the application collects:

  • Links to your music elsewhere (optional), such as SoundCloud, YouTube or Bandcamp pages.
  • A phone number, pre-filled from your profile if you added one there. It is kept with the application and does not change your profile.
  • A selfie and a photo of an identity document, such as an ID card or passport, chosen from your photo library.

We use these only to review your application: to confirm that you are a real person and the artist you say you are, to prevent impersonation and fraud, and to contact you about the application. The photos are reviewed by our staff; we do not use them for automated face matching or other biometric processing. The selfie and identity document are stored privately and can be viewed only by authorised Muse administrators. They are never shown to other users or published.

We keep an application, including its photos, links and phone number, for as long as your account exists, whether it was approved or not, so we can check a later application or a report of impersonation against it. When you delete your account, your applications and their photos are deleted with it (Section 9).

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs. The only third-party SDK that collects data for its own purposes is Google AdMob, and only for users who see ads (Section 2.6).
  • We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or camera.
  • We do not sell your personal information or share it with data brokers.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your data. We may share information only:

  • With service providers that host our backend and store content on our behalf, under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
  • With Google AdMob, which the App uses to show ads to users without Muse Plus, as described in Section 2.6.
  • With Apple or Google, to verify Muse Plus purchases.
  • For legal reasons, if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the service.
  • Public content: songs, albums, artist names, and profile photos that you publish as an artist are visible to other users by design.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. Artist applications, including the selfie and identity document photo, are kept as described in Section 2.7. You can delete your account at any time (see Section 9). When an account is deleted, we keep a minimal record of the deletion (the account's internal ID, the date, how many songs and albums were removed, and any balance forfeited) with no other personal data. Some information, such as withdrawal records, may be retained where required for legal, tax, security, or fraud-prevention reasons.

7. Security

Data transmitted between the App and our servers is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Authentication tokens are stored in encrypted on-device storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children's Privacy

Muse is not directed to children under the age required by your local law to consent to data processing (for example, 13 in the United States). We do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your account data by contacting us.
  • Account deletion: in the App, go to Settings › Delete Account. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, your uploaded songs and albums (with their files), your playlists, your saved payout details, and your artist applications (with their selfie and identity document photos). If you are an artist with money in your balance, you must withdraw it first; deletion is not possible while a withdrawal is pending. A balance below the minimum withdrawal amount cannot be paid out and is forfeited when you delete your account; the App shows the amount and asks you to confirm before deleting. See our Terms of Service. If you can't use the App, email us and we will process the request.
  • Advertising choices: see Section 2.6.
  • Depending on your region (e.g., EEA/UK or California), you may have additional rights such as data portability or objection to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these.

10. International Users

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own. By using the App, you consent to such transfer, subject to appropriate safeguards.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App.
