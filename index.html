Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No — you can browse and play music as a guest. Sign in to build playlists, save favorites, and follow artists.
  • How do I delete my account or data? In the app, go to Settings › Delete Account. Deleting also removes your uploads, and a balance below the minimum withdrawal is forfeited — see the Terms. If you can't use the app, email us at the address above.
  • I'm an artist — how do I upload music? Sign in, open your profile, and use the upload option to add songs and albums.
  • Playback stopped in the background. Make sure Muse has permission to play audio in the background and isn't being closed by battery optimization settings.
